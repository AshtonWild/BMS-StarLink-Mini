5S 26650 BMS (Starlink Mini / IP2368) - DESIGN REVIEW NOTES
============================================================
Written:        2026-11-24
Board revision: Rev 2026-11-03 (files last changed 2026-11-04)
Board checks:   0 DRC violations, 0 unconnected pads, schematic and PCB match

This file records the review findings, the part changes that bring an
already-manufactured board up to date, the design-file changes for a
future revision, and where to buy the replacement parts.

No design files were changed by this review.


1. FIX BEFORE USE (both are part swaps - no new PCB needed)
------------------------------------------------------------

1.1  U1 - BM3451TJDC-T28A is obsolete
     Fit BM3451TNDC-T28A (preferred) or BM3451UNDC-T28A instead.
     Same pads, same orientation, no other parts change.

       Version   Overcharge   Low-voltage   Balance
                 cut-off      cut-off       starts
       TJDC      4.250 V      2.5 V         4.190 V   (original, obsolete)
       TNDC      4.250 V      2.8 V         4.190 V   (preferred)
       UNDC      4.235 V      2.8 V         4.180 V   (also fine)

     DO NOT use HEDC or BHDC versions (3.65-3.85 V cut-off, wrong for
     Li-ion) or any -T20B version (different chip).

1.2  D1 - change SMCJ24CA (two-way) to SMCJ24A (one-way)
     Why: when the BMS cuts off a hard short, cable inductance pushes
     PACK- upward. The two-way TVS does not clamp until PACK- is about
     27-39 V above PACK+. That puts roughly 48-60 V across Q7 (rated
     40 V) and about -30 V on Q6's gate (believed rated +/-20 V).
     A one-way TVS clamps at about 1 V and holds Q7 to about 24 V.

     *** ORIENTATION WARNING ***
     The board was laid out for a two-way part, so the cathode mark on
     the board is on the WRONG side for the SMCJ24A.
       - Cathode (the end with the band) goes on the pad connected to
         PACK+. D1 sits beside the PACK- wire pad; the PACK+ pad of
         D1 is the one FARTHER from that wire pad.
       - The other pad connects to PACK-.
       - Fitted backwards, it shorts the pack when the output turns on.
     Check BEFORE connecting cells, multimeter in diode mode:
       - red on PACK-, black on PACK+  -> about 0.5-0.7 V
       - red on PACK+, black on PACK-  -> open
     If JLCPCB assembles the boards, leave D1 off the assembly order
     and solder it by hand. If boards arrive with the SMCJ24CA fitted,
     desolder it and fit the SMCJ24A.


2. SAFETY GAPS TO COVER IN THE BUILD
------------------------------------------------------------

2.1  No protection against charging below 0 C
     The BM3451 only has over-temperature cut-offs (about 45 C charge,
     62 C discharge with R22 = 8.66k). Charging Li-ion below freezing
     damages cells and can make them unsafe.
     Fix: wire a thermistor from the IP2368 board's NTC pin to the
     cells, and confirm its low-temperature cut-off in the IP2368
     datasheet.

2.2  TH1 does not touch a cell
     It sits on the top of the board between the cell 3 and cell 4 wire
     pads, so it reads board temperature.
     Fix: leave the 0603 part off. Solder a leaded 10k NTC to the TH1
     pads on two short wires and tape it to a middle cell. No polarity.

2.3  Battery wires have no strain relief
     The 2.5 mm2 wires solder to surface pads only; a tug can peel a
     pad off.
     Fix: after soldering, bond each wire to the board a few mm back
     from the pad with neutral-cure silicone or epoxy.

2.4  Cell insulation
     If the cells rest against the board, put fish paper or Kapton
     between them and the board.


3. OPTIONAL PART CHANGES (same pads)
------------------------------------------------------------

3.1  R2: 10 Mohm -> 5.1 Mohm. Halves the charge-FET turn-off time
     (about 0.15 s -> 0.08 s). Datasheet minimum is 5 Mohm.
3.2  C7, C8, C9 (100 nF) and C10 (22 nF): use +/-5% parts instead of
     +/-10%. Only improves the accuracy of the protection delays.


4. TEST ON THE BENCH BEFORE TRUSTING THE PACK
------------------------------------------------------------

4.1  D1 orientation check (see 1.2) before connecting any cells.
4.2  Wake-up after deep discharge. With R24 = 10 Mohm the BMS stays off
     after a low-voltage cut-off until a charger is applied. Run one
     cell down to the cut-off and confirm that plugging USB-C into the
     IP2368 brings the pack back. Not confirmed from datasheets.
4.3  Plug the Starlink / IP2368 into a live pack and confirm the
     short-circuit protection does not trip on the inrush.
4.4  There are no test points. Probe on component pads, e.g. the
     cell-tap resistors R6, R9, R11, R15, R18.


5. DESIGN-FILE CHANGES FOR A FUTURE BOARD REVISION
------------------------------------------------------------

     footprint 180 degrees so the board marking is correct.

5.3  Add zip-tie holes or a slot beside each of the 12 wire pads.
5.4  Add test points: five cell taps, CO, DO, GND, PACK-.
5.5  TH1: replace the 0603 footprint with two wire pads for a leaded
     thermistor.
5.6  Update R2 and the C7-C10 tolerances in the schematic and BOM.
5.7  Add the BM3451 symbol library to the project library table
     (clears the one remaining ERC warning).
5.8  After any change: re-run DRC and re-export the jlcpcb/ files.

7. CHECKED AND FINE
------------------------------------------------------------
- U1 wiring matches the BM3451 datasheet 5-cell circuit: cell filters
  (1k / 1 uF), VCC (1k / 10 uF), SET floating, DOIN/COIN pull-ups.
- Delays: overcharge 1 s, over-discharge 1 s, overcurrent-1 200 ms,
  overcurrent-2 4.4 ms.
- Trip currents with the 5 mohm shunt (R23): 20 A / 80 A / 160 A
  discharge, 10 A charge. Normal load is about 7 A (0.25 W in R23).
- Balancing: about 89 mA per cell, 0.37 W in 0.75 W resistors.
- Fuse F1 (25 A): distributor listing gives 500 A at 72 V DC
  interrupting rating.
- Layout: Kelvin current sensing, tented vias, 2 oz copper, 1.6 mm
  board, at least 0.3 mm between power copper areas.
